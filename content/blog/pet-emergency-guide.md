# Pet Emergency Guide: What to Do Before the Vet

1. **Call the clinic** while you’re en route — oxygen, surgery, and antidotes are not universal.
2. **Do not induce vomiting** for toxins unless a vet tells you to.
3. **Bring packaging or photos** of anything your pet ate.
4. **Keep your pet warm and as calm as possible** during transport.
