# Choosing a Dog Groomer: Red Flags to Watch For

Walk away if you smell **urine or rot** in the drying area, see **reused dirty towels**, or staff **force-restrain** every dog the same way. Good groomers explain mat shaving, ear cleaning, and nail length before they start.

Ask for **before/after photos** from their own clients, not stock images.
