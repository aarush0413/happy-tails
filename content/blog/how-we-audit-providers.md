# How We Audit Pet Service Providers in Pune

Happy Tails is not a paid directory. Every **LEGIT**, **CAUTION**, **WEAK**, or **BLACKLISTED** label is earned through research: public reviews, licensing where available, facility notes, and conversations with pet parents when we can reach them.

We cross-check inflated review counts, chain-wide ratings claimed as local, and serious incidents (police cases, council actions) before we publish.

If something changes after we publish, [email us](mailto:hello@happytails.in) — we update verdicts when the facts change.
