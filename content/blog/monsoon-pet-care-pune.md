# Monsoon Pet Care: Ticks & Waterborne Risks in Pune

Ticks explode after the first heavy weeks — check paws, ears, and groin daily. Stagnant water means **leptospirosis** risk for dogs that drink from puddles; keep fresh water in the car on walks.

Dry bedding matters: damp coats and floors invite skin infections, especially for short-nosed breeds.
